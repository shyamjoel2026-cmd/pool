=== hyderabad-possession-pipeline
BOTTOM: ANAROCK projects 63,700 Hyderabad homes scheduled for completion in 2026, out of 5.40 lakh across the top 7 cities, the biggest pipeline in over a decade. Delays are large, though: ANAROCK counts about 63,000 delayed Hyderabad units as of June 2026. A realistic count is about 32,000 to 54,000 actual handovers a year (my estimate). That works out to roughly Rs 310 to 1,130 crore a year of AC, fridge, washing machine and TV spend at move-in, with a central case of about Rs 500 to 820 crore (estimate, arithmetic in the findings). TG-RERA publishes project records and lets anyone reproduce its material free if the source is credited. Its search does filter by 'Proposed Date of Completion', but every search needs a CAPTCHA and I found no bulk export. So a lawful possession radar has to be RERA lookups done by people plus builder handover schedules plus licensed data. Automated scraping that gets past the CAPTCHA is not an option. Builders' own handover dates are the most reliable signal, because RERA completion dates slip by 9 to 12 months.

- [FACT/medium] The law requires public, quarterly-updated project data, but compliance is patchy. RERA s.34(b) requires the authority to publish a public website of project records. s.11(1) requires promoters to update bookings and project status quarterly. s.19(10) requires allottees to take possession within 2 months of the Occupancy Certificate (OC). :: FACT: s.11(1)(b) to (e) require quarterly lists of apartments booked, approvals taken and pending, and project status on the promoter's page on the authority's website (Indian Kanoon). TG-RERA sets QPR deadlines of 15 Apr, 15 Jul, 15 Oct and 15 Jan. It issued show-cause notices for non-filing on 17 May 2024, 20 May 2024 and 25 Sep 2024, and says some promoters are still non-compliant (per 1-comply and search summaries of TG-RERA circulars). s.19(10): 'Every allottee shall take physical possession... within a period of two months of the occupancy certificate'. So the OC date is the hard trigger for a 60-day buying window. OC status in Hyderabad appears to be trackable only by file number on TG-bPASS; I found no public bulk OC list. (https://indiankanoon.org/doc/4686580/ ; https://ibclaw.in/section-34-of-real-estate-regulation-and-development-act-2016-rera-functions-of-authority/ ; https://ibclaw.in/section-19-of-real-estate-regulation-and-development-act-2016-rera-rights-and-duties-of-allottees/ ; https://1-comply.com/telangana-rera-mandates-timely-submission-of-qprs-and-audit-reports/ ; https://www.sobha.com/blog/check-occupancy-certificate-online-ghmc/)
- [FACT/high] TG-RERA's copyright policy lets anyone reproduce its material free, provided it is accurate and credited. Legal exposure comes from how the data is collected: bypassing the CAPTCHA or mass extraction 'without permission' could fall under IT Act s.43(b). :: FACT: TG-RERA Disclaimer and Policies: 'Material featured on this portal may be reproduced free of charge in any format or media without requiring specific permission', subject to accurate reproduction, no derogatory or misleading use, and prominent acknowledgement of the source. Third-party copyright material is excluded. The policies say nothing about scraping. IT Act s.43(b) imposes civil liability on anyone who 'downloads, copies or extracts any data' from a computer system without the owner's permission. DPDP Act s.3(c)(ii) excludes personal data made public by a person under a legal obligation, which likely covers RERA promoter disclosures (legal interpretation, not a ruling). JUDGMENT: the lawful route is human-entered lookups (staff solve the CAPTCHA), written data-sharing permission from TG-RERA, RTI requests, or licensed vendor data. Do not build automated CAPTCHA bypass. (https://rera.telangana.gov.in/Home/DisclaimerPolicies ; https://indiankanoon.org/doc/39800/ ; https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-dpdp-act-publicly-available-personal-data-46899.html)

Delivery rate: 50% low (given the ~63,000-unit delay count), 70% central, 85% high (2024 top-7 actual). Move-in-and-buy share is my assumption, not sourced: 60% / 70% / 80%. IMPL: The pipeline is real but late. Plan on about 32,000 to 54,000 actual Hyderabad handovers a year, not 63,700. Run cohorts off builder-confirmed handover or OC dates, never the RERA 'proposed completion' date, which is slipping 9 to 12 months. | Build the possession radar in three layers. (1) TG-RERA lookups filtered by 'Proposed Date of Completion', done by the 4 field or ops staff typing the CAPTCHA themselves. (2) The founder's builder contacts for real handover and OC schedules. (3) Optionally, licensed PropEquity or CRE Matrix completion data. Do not write a scraper that gets past the CAPTCHA (IT Act s.43(b) risk). Credit TG-RERA as the source as its copyright policy requires, and consider asking TG-RERA in writing for bulk data or filing an RTI request. | Use the OC as the go-live trigger. RERA s.19(10) gives allottees 2 months from the OC to take possession, so run the sealed bid and fix the community price in that window. | Focus field effort on West and North-West Hyderabad (Tellapur, Kollur, Kokapet, Miyapur). This is where 56% of launches and 65% of inventory sit, and where single projects of 1,700 to 3,800 units exist. A handful of mega-projects can each fill a pool. | The basket is premium and AC-heavy: 2 to 3 ACs make up about 42 to 47% of basket value. Prioritise AC dealers and installation capacity.

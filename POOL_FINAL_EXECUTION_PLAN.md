## 2. What POOL sells and what must be proved

The buyer proposition is: **tell POOL the actual product you intend to buy; compare complete, credible offers from capable merchants; choose independently; pay the chosen merchant.** The merchant proposition is access to qualified, relevant purchase needs with enough information to quote economically and less wasted acquisition work.

POOL coordinates demand and evidence. It does not promise the cheapest price everywhere, guaranteed savings, guaranteed merchant performance, pooled custody of money, or a rebate funded by future volume. Buyer participation creates no purchase obligation. A valid accepted quote remains valid for one buyer even when everyone else leaves.

Two hypotheses remain separate:

1. **Demand Visibility Advantage, Mechanism A:** showing a merchant genuine, qualified, addressable demand improves its valid quantity-one price or objectively specified service offer relative to the defined comparator. A public-price saving alone does not prove this.
2. **Merchant Economic Acceptance, Mechanism B:** actual attributable business, fulfillment burden, retained economics, repeat participation and payment of real POOL fees justify the channel for that merchant. Interviews, clicks and orders alone cannot prove it; actual merchant profit may remain unknown.

A useful comparison/concierge service without evidence for A must be reported honestly. Do not market aggregation as proven because buyers found a cheaper shop. If the service only survives through founder persuasion, hidden subsidies or unrecovered labor, it is not ready to scale.

**Revenue decision:** retain the master's merchant-specific fixed fee on a correctly settled order. No buyer membership fee, paid rankings, contact resale, lead fee, assumed take rate or universal merchant fee is added. Research asks existing fee-willingness questions without charging. Signed commercial terms, tax treatment and actual amounts belong to G4 readiness.

## 3. Scope: broad discovery, selective activation

### 3.1 Initial universe

The four metros are concurrent initial discovery markets. None is relabeled a future expansion city. The universe is physical consumer products with a documented current comparable transaction value approximately **INR 5,000 or more**. This is an intake boundary, not evidence that a product supports pooling or belongs in the live pilot.

Use current selling/landed-price evidence. MRP alone and invented bundles do not qualify. Uncertain identity, price, availability or comparability is **UNKNOWN / NEEDS REVIEW**. Record observed value; do not invent value-band cutoffs or infer the buyer's spending ceiling.

Products enter research without an assumed appliance-only restriction. A category requiring material additional handling, expertise or review can be discovered and parked; discovery does not authorize its commercial sale. The master's exclusions for live used/refurbished/open-box goods, uncertain warranty, unavailable products and speculative substitutions remain intact. Nonphysical services are outside the initial universe.

### 3.2 How a lane earns attention

A lane is city × product/category, with its identity/comparability method, merchant capability, locality/service boundary and time window. A city or category name alone is not a lane specification.

**DECIDED POLICY:** permit broad intake but carry no more than **two deep-discovery lanes per city at once**, eight across the program. This is a work-in-progress cap, not a catalogue restriction or requirement to fill eight slots. A lane needs a genuine need or credible discovery lead, an identifiable comparison method and a plausible route to capability evidence. Do not fabricate representative SKUs to complete a table.

Within that cap, prioritize lanes with verifiable identity, transparent mandatory costs, independently controlled merchants, plausible pricing discretion and manageable fulfillment/remedy complexity. Use written reasons against those dimensions; no invented score claims that a city/category is objectively best. Preserve parked and failed candidates and their reasons. Replacing a lane consumes the same cash/time/contact caps and is logged before new collection.

Record the master's progression: discovered → evidence collected → comparability reviewed → merchant capability established → research lane → experiment eligible → live eligible after gates. These are evidence stages, not implemented application states.

### 3.3 Addressability and reporting

Cross-city demand reaches a merchant only when evidence shows it can and will serve that composition on the relevant terms. Cross-category volume matters only where the merchant can fulfill and economically values it. Preserve city, product lane, ownership cluster, window and exposure. Never add overlapping merchant views to create platform demand. Report every initial city, including no-access, no-supply and negative results.

## 12. Experiment decisions and thresholds

The present cycle is descriptive discovery. C01–C12 are deliberately deferred until real capability/ownership/request inputs exist; the founder owns that deferral. No experimental data collection starts simply because a protocol template exists.

For the subsequent run, complete every source preregistration field: C01 identity/objective/authority; C02 study classification/feasibility; C03 eligibility/roster; C04 full exposure history; C05 stable ownership-cluster allocation and prospective exclusions; C06 exact windows; C07 scripts/equal effort; C08 constant fee policy; C09 primary outcomes/thresholds; C10 secondary outcomes/protections; C11 missingness/contamination/analysis; C12 stopping/decision/closeout. Default remains **DESCRIPTIVE FEASIBILITY STUDY**. Reviewer-supported feasibility is required before any stronger inference.

Retain stable cluster assignment for the whole comparison block. Related shops and repeated quotes are not independent samples. Recruitment that reveals pooled demand is part of treatment history. Unknown history is not clean history. Preserve assignment and all departures; do not drop inconvenient nonresponses or merchants after seeing prices. Keep deadlines, scripts, fee treatment and follow-up comparable across arms.

Primary reporting must show valid-offer coverage and qualifying-offer rate per eligible invitation together with comparable prices among valid responses and their selection limits. Nonresponse is nonqualifying for coverage, not a zero price. Missing/stale benchmark makes savings unknown. Show city/lane/cluster/window and uncertainty before a traceable aggregate. ## 14. Business lifecycle the implementation must preserve

The following is a navigation and acceptance checklist. The full controlling predicates, transitions and exceptions remain in master §§8–19 and §§27–30; they are incorporated rather than replaced with simplified new state machines.

1. **Qualify a genuine need.** Confirm product/variant/condition and allowed alternatives; deduplicate the purchase need; confirm timing and broad locality. WATCHING is excluded from qualified near-term demand. Reconfirm and remove expired/withdrawn demand. Do not infer willingness-to-pay.
2. **Establish comparable evidence.** Use EXACT/EQUIVALENT/POSSIBLE/NOT_COMPARABLE deliberately. Record price, seller, availability, time, rights, warranty and mandatory delivery/installation/access costs. Conditional benefits remain separate. Unknown charge is not zero; MRP is not the benchmark; unpaid rebate is not realized saving.
3. **Verify merchant capability.** Identity and trading/GST evidence where applicable, actual authority, ownership links, relevant warranty/channel support, stock, invoice, serviceability and verified beneficiary. Registration alone does not prove brand authorization. AI extraction and seller assertion do not establish truth.
4. **Invite and quote privately.** Route only relevant addressable demand; preserve exposure. Capture full terms, capacity and expiry. No demand guarantee, volume-contingent accepted price, bid disclosure or paid ranking.
5. **Validate and present.** Reject/pause incomplete or invalid offers. Show guaranteed landed price separately from conditional benefits; disclose verification scope and limitations, terms, delivery, warranty, remedy and support. Show legitimate permitted external alternatives. Buyer may choose elsewhere without becoming a POOL order.
6. **Accept safely.** Verify buyer authority, offer version, stock/reservation and beneficiary; preserve immutable accepted snapshot. Enforce one active order per intent and shared merchant capacity across bids. Last-stock races must not create two valid reservations. Unilateral later merchant repricing is prohibited.
7. **Pay the merchant.** Buyer pays only the verified merchant-controlled route after reservation checks. POOL holds no purchase funds. Independently reverify changed beneficiary instructions. A receipt image or buyer assertion is not verified merchant receipt. UNKNOWN remains a hold; never advise blind repeat payment after uncertainty.
8. **Fulfill and remedy.** Merchant delivers the correct unit and required services; evidence records actual dates, identity/condition, invoice and required installation. Timely requests keep their actual received time even if processed later. Returns, replacements, refunds and warranty cases remain tracked; a replacement is not a second sale. No response is not successful fulfillment or refund.
9. **Settle only on all predicates.** Apply every §17 requirement: accepted terms, verified payment, correct delivery and required services, actual applicable maturity, no unresolved remedy/fraud hold, invoice, reconciliation and authorized verification. Timer expiry alone cannot settle an order.
10. **Recognize fees and correct history.** One original settlement and fixed fee assessment per original order. Later rescission/concession/correction uses linked adjustments and changes current net economics; history and accepted policy versions are preserved. Full reversal credits the original fee under the master's rule; invoice credit and actual cash refund remain separate.

Keep PURCHASE_PAYMENT, REVERSAL, ADJUSTMENT, SERVICE_REFUND, POOLBACK_PAYMENT, WARRANTY_REMEDY, FEE and ERROR_RECOVERY distinct according to the master's exact contracts. Never infer a merchandise reversal from every outward cash movement. Frozen program assessments and current net settlement are separately labeled.

## 15. Economics and the scorecard

Show separate counts and values for platform qualified demand, city qualified demand, product-lane demand, merchant-addressable demand, accepted orders, verified paid orders, delivered orders, current net-settled orders and any explicitly frozen program snapshot. None substitutes for the next. Cross-city/category rollups retain composition and excluded/missing records.

For each live lane and merchant, report actual fee agreed/invoiced/credited/collected, quote honor, repeat participation at the real fee, fulfillment/remedy/support effort, merchant refusals and willingness to continue. Self-reported incrementality is labeled. Neither buyer conversion nor merchant revenue alone proves profitable incremental business.

**Operating contribution view:** retained POOL fee revenue under the applicable recognition policy, less attributable acquisition, verification, fulfillment coordination, support, credits/losses borne by POOL and measured operating labor. Show cash receipts separately from accounting revenue and cash spending separately from unpaid labor. Disclose setup/shared costs and allocation, failed work, missing amounts and the cost of founder intervention. Do not turn unknown costs into zero or use GMV as POOL revenue.

The commercial fee must make sense on both sides: sufficient expected retained fee to cover POOL's attributable costs and support risk, while leaving the merchant willing to honor and repeat the business. If no feasible overlap exists at observed service quality, reduce avoidable work, change the tested lane prospectively or stop. Do not hide the gap in a universal commission percentage or future volume assumption.

Retention starts as two observable questions: do merchants submit another comparable bid at the disclosed fee, and do buyers return/referral-recruit a genuine need? Stated intention is reported separately. Broad product coverage may support repeat use; it does not establish purchase frequency or lifetime value.
